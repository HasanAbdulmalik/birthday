Chapter II

My Best Friend

Ayya, I call you my aunt because that’s what the family tree says, but it has never been the whole truth. You are my best friend. You’re the first person I think of when something good happens, and the first one I reach for when something doesn’t.

Through every high, you were louder about it than I was. Through every low, you were quiet in exactly the right way: no lectures, no hurry, just the door open and a place to sit. You’ve heard things I couldn’t say to anyone else, and you never once made me feel small for them.

You’re the one person I never have to explain myself to.

A good friend celebrates you. A great one tells you the truth, gently, and stays afterwards. You have done both for as long as I can remember, and I don’t think I’ve ever properly said how much that has held me up.

So this is my small attempt. A book, because books are your language. Every page is a thank-you: for the late talks, the honest advice, the laughter at the worst possible moments, and the way you make a hard day feel survivable.

Thank you for being my safe place.
